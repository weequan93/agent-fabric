The fixture makes direct calls to the original installed owners/functions at their declared seams; spies additionally record calls but cannot replace execution. In-memory or SQLite test owners are explicitly tagged synthetic. A missing/incompatible installed owner fails the affected local composition; do not copy its authority implementation to obtain green. P separately establishes the real persistence and transaction behavior.

Cases tagged L+X/P have two executions/evidence records. L proves only its fixture/refusal portion; P or X remains outstanding until actually run. All tables inherit the fresh fixture precondition above. Their data column gives additional required fixtures/state. **Levels** are unit, integration, e2e, performance or security; concurrency/recovery are scenario properties, not substitute levels.

## 11. Concurrency, recovery and measurement protocol

Concurrency uses explicit IPC/HTTP barriers and observed persisted state, never a sleep assumed to create a race. Record client PID/birth/nonce, command/operation ID, generation, resource and observer timestamps.
